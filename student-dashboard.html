Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my vote after submission?

No. Once you submit your vote, it cannot be changed. Please review carefully before submitting.

Who can vote in this election?

Only verified students can vote. You must have a valid student email and index number.

How many times can I vote?

You can only vote once per election. The system prevents duplicate votes.

What if I forget my login details?

Use your KTU email and index number to login. If you need further assistance, contact elections@ktu.edu.gh

Are my votes secure and confidential?

Yes. All votes are encrypted and stored securely. Your voting details are confidential.
